Doctor suggestions

Doctors review donors and leave a fitness verdict. Patients see it on every donor card.

Before donating

  • Age 18 to 65, weight at least 50 kg.
  • Hemoglobin at least 12.5 g/dL.
  • Eat a meal and drink plenty of water.
  • Sleep well the night before.

After donating

  • Rest 10 to 15 minutes and have a snack.
  • Avoid heavy exercise for 24 hours.
  • Keep the bandage on for several hours.
  • Wait at least 3 months before donating again.

Do not donate if

  • You have a fever, infection or cold.
  • You had surgery, tattoo or piercing in the last 6 months.
  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • You take blood thinners or have a serious illness.
